2. What is Prompt Engineering? 🛠️

🍕 Ordering Pizza

"Pizza please" gets you some pizza. "Medium, thin crust, extra cheese, no onions" gets you your pizza. Prompts work the same way!

🧭 Giving Directions

"Go that way" is confusing. "Turn left at the temple, go 200 m, it's the blue gate" is clear. Be the clear guide.

🎯 Why it matters

Good prompts save time, reduce mistakes, and make AI outputs repeatable for real products.
