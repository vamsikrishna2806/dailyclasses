# Stock Agent — Plain English Guide

## What does this agent do?

This agent looks up current stock prices for you. Ask it about any publicly traded company and it will show you the latest price, how much it has changed today, and other key market statistics.

> ⚠️ **This is for information only. It is not financial advice. Never make investment decisions based solely on this tool.**

## How to talk to it

Just ask naturally:

- "What is Apple's stock price?"
- "How is Tesla doing today?"
- "Show me the price of MSFT"
- "What is Amazon's current share price?"
- "How has Google stock moved today?"

## How it works (simple version)

1. You ask about a company or stock symbol
2. The agent looks up the latest data from Yahoo Finance
3. It returns the current price, daily change, and other figures
4. It presents them in a clear table

## What you get back

- **Current Price** — the latest traded price
- **Today's Change** — how much it has gone up or down today (in $ and %)
- **Trading Volume** — how many shares have been traded today
- **52-Week High** — the highest price in the last year
- **52-Week Low** — the lowest price in the last year

> *Data from Yahoo Finance. Not financial advice.*

## Good to know

- Works for **US stocks** and many international markets
- Completely free — no account or API key needed
- You can use the **company name** ("Apple") or the **ticker symbol** ("AAPL")
- Data may be delayed by 15–20 minutes (standard Yahoo Finance delay)

## Limitations

- Not real-time — prices may be delayed up to 20 minutes
- Not for making investment decisions
- Crypto, forex, and options are not covered (stock equities only)
