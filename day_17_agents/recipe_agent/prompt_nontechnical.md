# Recipe Agent — Plain English Guide

## What does this agent do?

This agent is your personal cooking assistant. Ask it how to cook any dish and it will find the recipe for you — including every ingredient with the exact amount needed and step-by-step cooking instructions.

## How to talk to it

Just ask in plain English:

- "How do I make pasta carbonara?"
- "What are the ingredients for chocolate cake?"
- "Show me a biryani recipe"
- "How do I cook butter chicken?"

## How it works (simple version)

1. You ask about a dish
2. The agent searches a free recipe database (TheMealDB)
3. It gets the ingredients and cooking steps
4. It presents them in a clear, easy-to-follow format

## What you get back

- The name of the dish
- A full list of ingredients with measurements
- Numbered cooking steps in order
- Optional cooking tips

## Good to know

- It knows **300+ dishes** from around the world
- Works best with the **dish name** (e.g., "pizza", "biryani", "tiramisu")
- If a dish isn't found, try a more common spelling or a similar dish name
- It uses a **completely free** database — no account needed

## Example conversation

> **You:** How do I make guacamole?
>
> **Agent:** Here's how to make Guacamole:
>
> **Ingredients:**
> - 2 avocados (ripe)
> - 1 lime (juiced)
> - 1 tsp salt
> - ½ cup onion (diced)
>
> **Instructions:**
> 1. Cut avocados and scoop into a bowl
> 2. Mash with a fork until chunky
> 3. Add lime juice and salt...

## Limitations

- Only covers dishes in the TheMealDB database
- Ingredient quantities are approximate — taste and adjust!
- Does not handle dietary substitutions (ask it directly though — it will try to help)
