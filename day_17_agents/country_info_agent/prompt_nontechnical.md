# Country Info Agent — Plain English Guide

## What does this agent do?

This agent is like a mini encyclopaedia for countries. Ask it about any country and it will give you a quick profile: capital city, population, languages spoken, currency, and more.

## How to talk to it

Just ask naturally:

- "Tell me about Japan"
- "What is the capital of Brazil?"
- "What language do they speak in Switzerland?"
- "What currency does Kenya use?"
- "How big is Australia?"

## How it works (simple version)

1. You ask about a country
2. The agent looks it up in the RestCountries database
3. It retrieves key facts
4. It presents them in a clear, easy-to-read format

## What you get back

- **Capital city**
- **Population**
- **Area** (in km²)
- **Region** (e.g., Asia, Europe, Africa)
- **Official languages**
- **Currency** (name and symbol)
- **Country code** (ISO)

> **You:** Tell me about France
>

> | Capital | Paris |
> | Population | 67,391,582 |

> | Region | Europe |
> | Languages | French |

> France is Western Europe's largest country, home to the Eiffel Tower and world-renowned cuisine.

## Good to know

- Covers **250+ countries and territories**
- Completely free, no account needed
- Works with country names in English
- If you ask about an ambiguous name (like "Congo"), it will clarify which one

## Limitations

- Population and area figures may not reflect the very latest census
- Disputed territories may have limited data
