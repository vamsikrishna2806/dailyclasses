# Wikipedia Agent — Plain English Guide

## What does this agent do?

This agent searches Wikipedia for you and gives you a clear, concise summary on any topic. Ask it about people, places, historical events, scientific concepts, or anything else you're curious about.

## How to talk to it

Just ask naturally:

- "Who is Marie Curie?"
- "What is quantum physics?"
- "Tell me about the Eiffel Tower"
- "What happened during the French Revolution?"
- "Explain what DNA is"
- "Who was Nelson Mandela?"

## How it works (simple version)

1. You ask about any topic
2. The agent searches Wikipedia
3. It retrieves a focused summary (about 5 sentences)
4. It explains it clearly and includes the Wikipedia link

## What you get back

- A clear explanation of the topic
- Key facts and context
- A link to the full Wikipedia article for further reading

## Example conversation

> **You:** What is the International Space Station?
>
> **Agent:** The International Space Station (ISS) is a modular space station in low Earth orbit. It is a multinational collaborative project between NASA, Roscosmos, JAXA, ESA, and CSA. The first component was launched in 1998 and the station has been continuously occupied since 2000. It serves as a research laboratory and orbits at approximately 400 km altitude at 28,000 km/h.
>
> 📖 Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Space_Station

## Good to know

- Covers **millions of topics** — if it's on Wikipedia, this agent can find it
- Completely free, no account needed
- Works best with specific names or terms
- Always includes the source link so you can read more

## Limitations

- Summaries are short — click the link for the full article
- Very recent events (last few days) may not be in Wikipedia yet
- Some niche or very local topics may have limited Wikipedia coverage
