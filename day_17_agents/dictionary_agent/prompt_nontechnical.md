# Dictionary Agent — Plain English Guide

## What does this agent do?

This agent is your personal dictionary. Ask it about any English word and it will tell you what it means, how to pronounce it, give you example sentences, and list similar words (synonyms).

## How to talk to it

Just ask naturally:

- "What does ephemeral mean?"
- "Define the word resilience"
- "How do you pronounce ubiquitous?"
- "Give me synonyms for happy"
- "What part of speech is 'eloquent'?"

## How it works (simple version)

1. You ask about a word
2. The agent looks it up in the Free Dictionary database
3. It gets the definition, pronunciation, examples, and synonyms
4. It explains everything clearly

## What you get back

- **Pronunciation** — written out phonetically (e.g., /ɪˈfem.ər.əl/)
- **Part of speech** — noun, verb, adjective, etc.
- **Definition** — what the word means
- **Example sentences** — how the word is used in context
- **Synonyms** — other words with similar meanings

## Example conversation

> **You:** What does resilience mean?
>
> **Agent:** **Resilience** /rɪˈzɪl.i.əns/
>
> *noun*
>
> **Definition:** The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; mental toughness.
>
> **Example:** "She showed remarkable resilience after losing her job."
>
> **Synonyms:** toughness, adaptability, flexibility, durability

## Good to know

- Works for any English word — simple or advanced vocabulary
- Completely free, no account required
- If a word isn't recognised, try checking the spelling

## Limitations

- English language only
- Very new slang or brand-new words may not be in the database
- Technical jargon from specialist fields may have limited entries
