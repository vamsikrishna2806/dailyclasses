# Political Scenario Agent — Plain English Guide

---

## What Is This?

This is an AI-powered political analyst you can have a conversation with
on your own computer.

You ask it about any political event, country, election, or world issue —
and it **searches the live internet** to find the latest news, then explains
what's happening, who the key players are, what the different sides think,
and what might happen next.

It's like having a calm, well-read political commentator available any time,
one who always goes and checks the news before answering rather than
speaking from memory.

---

## Who Is It For?

- Students studying politics, history, or current affairs
- Teachers and educators preparing lessons on world events
- Journalists and writers researching background
- Business professionals monitoring geopolitical risks
- Anyone who wants to understand the news more deeply
- Curious people who follow world events

No technical knowledge needed. Just type and read.

---

## How to Use It

1. Open your terminal and start the program.
2. Type your question in plain English — as if you're asking a knowledgeable friend.
3. The assistant will search the web and come back with an analysis.
4. Keep the conversation going — ask follow-up questions.
5. Type `quit` when you're done.

## Example Conversations

**Understanding a conflict:**
```
You:   What is happening between India and Pakistan right now?

Agent: Based on current reporting from Reuters and Al Jazeera...

       SITUATION: Tensions remain elevated along the Line of Control
       in Kashmir following a series of skirmishes in October 2026.

       KEY PLAYERS:
         India   — pushing for stronger border controls and accusing
                   Pakistan-based groups of cross-border attacks.
         Pakistan — denying involvement and calling for UN mediation.

       DIFFERENT VIEWS:
         Indian government sees this as a security issue.
         Pakistani government frames it as a sovereignty dispute.
         International observers are calling for bilateral dialogue.

       LIKELY NEXT STEPS: Diplomatic back-channel talks are ongoing.
       A full escalation is considered unlikely given both nations'
       economic pressures.

       Sources: Reuters, Dawn, The Hindu
```

**Election analysis:**
```
You:   Who is likely to win the French elections?

Agent: Searching for the latest French election polling...

       According to polls from Le Monde and France24 as of this week,
       the race is currently tight between three main blocs...
       [continues with balanced analysis]
```

**Economic policy:**
```
You:   What are US-China trade tensions about and who does it affect?

Agent: [searches web, then explains tariffs, supply chains, and which
       countries and industries are most impacted — in plain language]
```

## Important Things to Know

**It is balanced, not opinionated.**
The agent is instructed to present multiple sides without personal bias.
It will not tell you who is "right" or "wrong" in a political dispute.

**It searches live news.**
Every answer is based on what it finds online at that moment, not on old
training data. This means you get current information.

**It always mentions its sources.**
The agent names the news outlets and websites it found the information from,
so you can go and read more yourself.

**It can search multiple times.**
For a complex question, it may search the web two or three times from
different angles before giving you its final answer.

---

## What It Cannot Do

- It cannot predict the future with certainty — only outline likely scenarios.
- It cannot access paywalled or subscription-only news sites.
- It may occasionally find outdated or inaccurate content — always verify
  important information from primary sources.
- It does not give legal, financial, or investment advice.
- It is not a replacement for professional political or risk analysis.

---

## Limitations to Know

| Situation | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Very obscure local event | May find limited sources |
| Paywalled article | Cannot read it, will use other sources |
| Very recent event (last few hours) | Search results may not reflect it yet |
| Highly sensitive or contested topic | Will present multiple views, not take sides |
