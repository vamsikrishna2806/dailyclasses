# Travel Assistant Agent — Plain English Guide

---

## What Is This?

This is a friendly travel assistant you can chat with on your computer.

You ask it about any place you're planning to visit — and it instantly checks
the **real, live weather** for that destination and tells you exactly what
to expect: how hot or cold it is, whether rain is likely, and what to pack.

It's like texting a well-travelled friend who always knows the weather.

---

## Who Is It For?

Anyone planning a trip — whether it's a holiday abroad, a weekend getaway,
a business visit, or just daydreaming about a destination.

No technical knowledge needed. Just type and read.

---

## How to Use It

1. Open your terminal and start the program.
2. Type your question in plain English.
3. Read the assistant's reply.
4. Keep chatting — ask about as many cities as you like.
5. Type `quit` when you're done.

---

## Example Conversations

**Packing advice:**
```
You:   I'm travelling to Paris next week. What should I pack?

Agent: Paris is currently 9°C and partly cloudy — cool autumn weather.
       Over the next 3 days expect 8–13°C with a 40% chance of rain
       on Wednesday.

       Pack: a warm jacket, a light waterproof layer, comfortable
       walking shoes, and a small umbrella. It's a great time to
       explore the Louvre and the Marais district without the summer
       crowds!
```

**Simple weather check:**
```
You:   What's the weather like in Dubai right now?

Agent: Dubai is currently 34°C (feels like 38°C) with clear skies.
       It will stay hot all week — highs of 35°C and very low chance
       of rain.

       Stay hydrated, wear light breathable clothing, and try to plan
       outdoor activities in the morning or evening to avoid the
       midday heat.
```

**Holiday planning:**
```
You:   Is Bali good to visit in November?

Agent: Bali is currently 28°C, partly cloudy, with moderate humidity.
       The 3-day forecast shows daily highs of 29°C but a 70% rain
       chance — November is the start of Bali's wet season.

       Pack light summer clothes plus a good rain jacket or poncho.
       Mornings are usually dry and perfect for temple visits or
       rice terrace walks before the afternoon showers roll in.
```

## What You Can Ask

Ask anything about a destination's weather and travel conditions:

| Question type | Example |
|---|---|
| Packing advice | "What should I pack for Iceland?" |
| Weather check | "How is the weather in Singapore today?" |
| Activity planning | "Is it good weather for hiking in Nepal?" |
| Season advice | "Is Bangkok hot right now?" |
| General trip prep | "Tell me about weather in New York this week" |

---

## What It Does Behind the Scenes
*(Just for the curious — you don't need this to use it)*

1. It understands your question using an AI model running on your computer.
2. It looks up live weather data from a free weather service.
3. It combines the weather facts with travel knowledge to give you advice.

No personal data is collected. No paid subscription needed. Works offline
for the AI part — only the weather lookup needs internet.

---

## What It Cannot Do

- It cannot book flights, hotels, or tours.
- It cannot check visa requirements or travel restrictions.
- It only gives weather-based advice — it doesn't know about local events
  or public holidays.
- It cannot predict weather more than 3 days ahead.

---

## In Simple Words

> "Tell me where you're going. I'll check the real weather there right now
> and tell you what to pack, what to expect, and how to make the most of it."
