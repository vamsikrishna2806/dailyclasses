# Fun Facts Agent — Plain English Guide

## What does this agent do?

This agent is your entertainment companion! It tells jokes and shares fascinating facts about numbers. Perfect for a laugh, a fun icebreaker, or satisfying your curiosity about random numbers.

## How to talk to it

Just ask in a casual, fun way:

- "Tell me a joke"
- "Give me a programming joke"
- "Tell me something interesting about the number 7"
- "What's special about the year 1969?"
- "Tell me a pun"
- "Make me laugh!"
- "Fun fact about 42"

## How it works (simple version)

1. You ask for a joke or a number fact
2. The agent picks the right tool (joke tool, number fact tool, or both!)
3. It fetches the content from free joke/trivia APIs
4. It delivers it with flair and enthusiasm

## The two tools

**Tool 1 — Joke Tool:**
- Gets random jokes from JokeAPI
- Categories: General, Programming, Puns, Spooky, Christmas, or Any

**Tool 2 — Number Facts Tool:**
- Gets interesting trivia about any number, year, or date
- Example: "1969 is the year of the first Moon landing"

## Example conversations

> **You:** Tell me a programming joke
>
> **Agent:** 😄 Why do programmers prefer dark mode?
>
> ...Because light attracts bugs! 🐛
>
> Want another one? Just ask!

---

> **You:** What's special about the number 42?
>
> **Agent:** 🔢 Here's a fun fact about 42: 42 is the answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything according to *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*!

## Good to know

- Completely free, no account needed
- If you ask for a **programming joke**, it will specifically give developer humour
- It can combine a joke AND a number fact in one response
- Keep it clean — the default setting avoids dark/mature content

## Limitations

- Joke quality varies (it's random!)
- Number facts are from a database — very unusual or negative numbers may have limited data
- It won't remember previous jokes to avoid repeats
