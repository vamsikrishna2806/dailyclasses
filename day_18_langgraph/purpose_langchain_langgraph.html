The purpose, in motion

What LangChain and LangGraph Do

Both tools help you build AI apps. LangChain's purpose is to connect the parts. LangGraph's purpose is to control the order those parts run in. Press the buttons to see each one.

LangChain

Its purpose: connect the parts

An AI app needs a model, a prompt, tools, and data. LangChain gives each part a standard shape, so they plug together like parts on a line.

Press Connect. Each part snaps into place.
In one line LangChain answers: what are the parts, and how do they connect?
LangGraph

Its purpose: control the order

Real work is not always a straight line. The next step can depend on the last result. LangGraph runs the steps as a map, so the app can decide, loop back, and wait for a person.

Press Run. Watch the order change as the answer changes.
In one line LangGraph answers: what runs next, when do we loop, and when do we stop or wait?

Side by side

LangChain Gives you the parts and a way to join them. Best when the order is always the same.
LangGraph Gives you the map: decisions, loops, shared state, pauses. Best when the next step depends on the result.

How they work together

LangGraph does not replace LangChain. Each step of the map can be a LangChain chain. LangGraph decides the order, and LangChain does the work inside each box.
