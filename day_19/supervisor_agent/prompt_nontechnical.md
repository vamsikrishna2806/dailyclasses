# Supervisor Agent — Plain English Guide

## What does this agent do?

This agent is like a **newsroom with a boss**. You give a topic, and the boss assigns the work: a researcher finds facts, a writer turns them into an article, and a proofreader polishes it. After each person finishes, they report back to the boss, who decides what happens next.

## How to talk to it

Type just a topic:

- "Black holes"
- "The Eiffel Tower"
- "Photosynthesis"

## How it works (simple version)

1. You give a topic
2. The **supervisor** (boss) sends the **researcher** to look it up on Wikipedia
3. The researcher reports back → the boss sends in the **writer**
4. The writer reports back → the boss sends in the **proofreader**
5. The boss sees everything is done and hands you the finished article

## The team

- **Supervisor** — plans and delegates, never writes
- **Researcher** — the only one with a tool (Wikipedia)
- **Writer** — turns notes into an ~80-word article
- **Proofreader** — fixes grammar and polish

## Example conversation

> **You:** Black holes
>
> *supervisor → researcher*
> *supervisor → writer*
> *supervisor → proofreader*
> *supervisor → FINISH*
>
> **Agent:** **Black Holes: Cosmic Enigmas** — A black hole is an astronomical body so dense that its gravity warps spacetime...

## Good to know

- Free — Wikipedia needs no key, and the AI runs on your own computer
- You can follow the boss's decisions as they are printed
- A safety limit stops the team from going in circles

## Limitations

- Needs an internet connection for Wikipedia
- Very obscure topics may have no Wikipedia page
- The article is short and only as accurate as the Wikipedia summary
